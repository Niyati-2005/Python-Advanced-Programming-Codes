# Q1 – Air Quality Data Analysis

## Objective

Analyze air quality data from multiple cities using Python. The analysis includes data cleaning, monthly PM2.5 averages, identification of the most polluted city, comparative visualizations, and correlation analysis.

## Dataset

- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Dataset:** Air Quality Data in India
- **URL:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rohanrao/air-quality-data-in-india

## Libraries Used

- pandas
- numpy
- matplotlib
- seaborn

## Analysis Performed

1. Loaded and inspected the dataset.
2. Checked for missing and duplicate values.
3. Converted the `Date` column to datetime format.
4. Handled missing values.
5. Calculated monthly average PM2.5 levels.
6. Identified the most polluted city based on average PM2.5.
7. Created comparative line charts for air pollution levels.
8. Generated a correlation heatmap for pollutant variables.
9. Interpreted the results and prepared a conclusion.

## Visualizations

The analysis includes:

- Monthly PM2.5 trend
- City-wise PM2.5 comparison
- Correlation heatmap

## Conclusion

The analysis helps identify pollution trends across cities and understand relationships between different air pollutants. The results can be used to compare air quality and identify cities with relatively higher pollution levels.
