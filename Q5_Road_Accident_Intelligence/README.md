# Q5 – Road Accident Analysis

## Objective

Analyze road accident data to understand accident patterns, severity, contributing factors, casualties, and other characteristics of road accidents.

## Dataset

- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Dataset:** Global Road Accidents Dataset
- **Dataset Size:** 132,000 records
- **Features:** 30

## Libraries Used

- pandas
- numpy
- matplotlib
- seaborn
- scikit-learn

## Analysis Performed

1. Loaded and inspected the dataset.
2. Checked data types and dataset dimensions.
3. Checked for missing values.
4. Checked for duplicate records.
5. Analyzed accident severity.
6. Analyzed accident causes.
7. Examined accident patterns by country and year.
8. Created visualizations to identify accident trends.
9. Analyzed injuries and fatalities.
10. Interpreted the major findings from the dataset.

## Visualizations

The analysis includes:

- Accident distribution by severity
- Accidents by country
- Accidents by year
- Accident causes
- Injuries and fatalities
- Other relevant accident trends

## Conclusion

The analysis provides insight into road accident patterns and the factors associated with accident severity, injuries, and fatalities. Visualization and statistical analysis help identify important trends in the dataset and provide a basis for understanding road safety patterns.
